HITCH

The daily word-chain puzzle

One word-chain puzzle a day. Every puzzle is a chain of words, and each neighboring pair forms a compound word or a familiar phrase. You see the first and last words. Fill in the hidden ones, revealing as few letters as you can.

KEY → HOLE → PUNCH → LINE → UP → STREAM

Keyhole, hole punch, punchline, lineup, upstream. Every link you get right snaps into place.
